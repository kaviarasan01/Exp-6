# EXP – 6: STUDENT ONLINE EXAM REGISTRATION SYSTEM
# AIM:

To analyze and design a Student Online Exam Registration System that allows students to register for examinations, view exam details, manage registrations, and receive confirmation of their exam registration.

# SRS (Procedure):
1.Identify the functional and non-functional requirements of the Student Online Exam Registration System.
2.Identify the main actors such as Student, Staff, ERP, and Admin.
3.Define the major functions including student login, viewing exam details, exam registration, registration confirmation, and administration.
4.Prepare the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) based on the identified requirements.
5.Design UML diagrams to represent the structure, behavior, and interactions of the system.
6.Verify the diagrams to ensure that they correctly represent the Student Online Exam Registration System.
